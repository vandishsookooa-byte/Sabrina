Calculation Methodology Reference

This page documents the formulas, data fields, and business rules used throughout the dashboard.

KPI Formula Data Fields Used Business Rules
Attendance % (Present Records / Total Records) × 100 hours_worked, leave_quantity, is_present Rows with hours_worked ≥ 2 are treated as Present, except rows with full-day leave (leave_quantity ≥ 1).
Absenteeism % (Absent Records / Total Records) × 100 status, leave_type, is_absent Only NA and UA absence markers are classified as Absenteeism.
Leave % (Approved Leave Quantity / Total Records) × 100 leave_type, leave_quantity, leave_quantity_approved, is_leave Only company-approved leave types are included in leave calculations.
OT % ((OT1 + OT2 + OT3) / (Total Records × 9)) × 100 ot1, ot2, ot3 Total OT % combines all overtime buckets and recalculates after every filter selection.
OT1 % (OT1 / (Total Records × 9)) × 100 ot1 Calculated per group (company/department/employee) after active filters are applied.
OT2 % (OT2 / (Total Records × 9)) × 100 ot2 Calculated per group (company/department/employee) after active filters are applied.
OT3 % (OT3 / (Total Records × 9)) × 100 ot3 Calculated per group (company/department/employee) after active filters are applied.
Missing Hours % (Missing Hours / (Total Records × 9)) × 100 missing_hours Missing hours are aggregated from attendance records and recalculated for all filters.
